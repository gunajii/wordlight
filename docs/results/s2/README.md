# S2 results — speech recognition in the reading loop

**Status: NOT MEASURED YET.** No real speech-recognition result exists until Amazon Transcribe is reachable from the
account (blocked on AWS credits, 2026-10-05). Nothing in this folder may be quoted until a run from a real engine lands
here.

| Engine | What it is | Where its results go | Counts as S2? |
|---|---|---|---|
| `scripted` | Local simulation. Transcripts come from each case's script, with fixed simulated timing. Checks the harness and the reading engine's strictness and normalisation. | `bench/runs/s2/` (not committed) | **No** — SIMULATED |
| `polly-transcribe` | Amazon Polly (adult synthetic voice) speaks each case. The audio is streamed in real time (40 ms, 16 kHz PCM16) to Amazon Transcribe Streaming. | here, `polly-transcribe-<date>/` | Yes, for the pipeline with synthetic adult speech |
| `wav` | Adult control recordings, or consented child recordings, stored **outside the repo** and streamed to Transcribe | here, `wav-<date>/` | Yes, for the speaker recorded |
| `score-observed.ts` | Live reading in the product, scored against an adult observer's sheet. No audio is recorded. | here, `observed-<date>/` | Yes, for the readers observed |

**Pass rule (fixed before any real run):**
- ≥ 90 % of correctly read words lit within 1 s of being spoken, and
- ≤ 10 % of deliberately misread words accepted, both per language.

PARTIAL means one language passes; FAIL means neither passes. On FAIL, the product switches to Echo Mode (`READING_MODE=echo`).
The matcher is not to be loosened to reach the target. The two engine changes made from the scripted runs are listed in
`docs/SPIKES.md` (S2); both are resynchronisation rules that never light a substituted word.

**Cases.** `tools/s2/cases.json`, generated by `tools/s2/make-cases.ts` from `tools/s2/lines.json` (original text).
There are 169 cases, 10 English and 10 Hindi lines, covering: correct, slip, missing, repeated, hesitation, long pause,
wrong word, multiple wrong, correction, Hindi Unicode and English edge cases.
