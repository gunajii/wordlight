## Narration audio
`content/stories/*/p*.mp3` and the help-word clips `content/stories/*/h*.mp3` were generated by the author with
Amazon Polly (voice “Kajal”; generative engine for English, neural for Hindi) from the story texts above. They are
included so the demo runs from a fresh clone; their use is subject to the AWS Service Terms for Amazon Polly. Rebuild
them with `node tools/content/build-story.ts <id>` (needs AWS credentials).
